Submit your product

Are you an independent developer? Getting listed is free, and submissions are reviewed by a human — usually within a few days.

What we look for

  • Built by an independent developer or a team of 1–3 people
  • A real, working product people can try today
  • Original work — no affiliate spam or adult content
  • Any pricing model is welcome: free, freemium, paid, or open source

FAQ

Everything about getting listed.

What counts as an independent developer product?

Products built primarily by one to three people, self-funded and independent of big companies. Both full-time indie hackers and side-project builders are welcome.

Does listing cost anything?

No. Listing on Indie Gems is completely free — no paid placement, no affiliate links, and editorial order is not for sale.

How do I submit my product?

Email tliens@163.com with your product name, link, one-line intro, maker and pricing model. The “Open email template” button above fills this in for you. Most reviews take a few days.

Can I update or remove my listing?

Yes — email the same address with the change. We can update the description, link, or pricing, and honor removal requests at any time.

How often is the directory updated?

New listings are added as they're reviewed — roughly weekly. The product count and cards on the front page update with each release.
